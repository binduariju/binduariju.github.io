**Authors:** **Riju Bindua**, Minati De, Naveen Garg, Kanav Singla

This paper grew out of my undergraduate thesis at IIT Delhi.

One of the things I find most fascinating about online algorithms is how dramatically the picture changes when we allow algorithms to occasionally revise earlier decisions. Even a very small amount of flexibility can completely change what is achievable.

In this work we study this phenomenon for geometric piercing problems. We design algorithms that remain highly competitive while making only a small amount of recourse, establish lower bounds showing when recourse is unavoidable, and extend these ideas from intervals to higher-dimensional geometric objects.
