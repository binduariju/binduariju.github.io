**Authors:** **Riju Bindua**, Hamed Hatami, Hasti Karimi, Robert Robere

Approximate sign rank is a recently introduced relaxation of the classical notion of sign rank, motivated by problems in communication complexity and learning theory. In this work we develop new lower-bound techniques based on convex geometry, establish new connections with VC dimension, and study the approximate sign rank of Hadamard matrices.

Our main technical contribution is a geometric **hyperplane avoidance theorem**, proved using the Forster–Barthe isotropic position theorem together with the Bourgain–Tzafriri restricted invertibility principle. As applications, we obtain polynomial lower bounds for approximate sign rank of geometric concept classes and show that Hadamard matrices do not provide polynomial lower bounds in the approximate setting.

### Abstract

We prove new upper and lower bounds on ϵ-approximate sign-rank, a relaxation of sign-rank introduced by Chornomaz, Moran, and Waknine (STOC 2025). We show that every \(m\times n\) sign matrix with approximate sign-rank \(d\) contains a monochromatic rectangle of size \(d^{-O(d)}m \times d^{-O(d^2)}n\), paralleling classical results for exact sign-rank. As an application, we establish a lower bound of \(\Omega(\sqrt{d/\log d})\) on the ϵ-approximate sign-rank of large-margin \(d\)-dimensional half-spaces.

A key ingredient is a new geometric theorem on hyperplane avoidance. We also establish connections between approximate sign rank and VC dimension, and prove new upper and lower bounds for the approximate sign rank of Hadamard matrices.
