**Authors:** **Riju Bindua**, Hamed Hatami, Hasti Karimi, Robert Robere

Approximate sign rank is a remarkably natural relaxation of the classical notion of sign rank, but surprisingly little was known about how closely the two notions are related. This project began with a simple question: *which of the beautiful structural results for sign rank continue to hold in this more forgiving setting?*

To answer this, we developed new lower-bound techniques by bringing together ideas from geometry, communication complexity, and learning theory. Along the way we prove a new hyperplane avoidance theorem, establish new connections with VC dimension, and show that Hadamard matrices—so central to the classical theory—behave quite differently for approximate sign rank.

This work is part of my current research at McGill.
