This seminar presented results from joint work with Hamed Hatami, Hasti Karimi, and Robert Robere on lower bounds for approximate sign rank.

The talk focused on a geometric question arising in this work: given a set of points in general position in \(\mathbb{R}^d\), can we find several large subsets such that no hyperplane simultaneously splits all of them? I discussed our hyperplane avoidance theorem and explained how it is used in the approximate sign-rank lower bound.
