This short presentation introduced a problem that we had begun studying as a joint project: how to compute zero-dimensional persistent homology for graph filtrations indexed by the \(D_4\) quiver rather than by a linear order.

At that stage, the aim was to present the problem and the questions it raised. The project eventually developed into joint work with Thomas Brüstle and Luis Scoccola on decomposing zero-dimensional persistent homology over rooted tree quivers.
