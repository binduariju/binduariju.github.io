Hello and welcome! I am a Computer Science PhD student at McGill University, where I am incredibly fortunate to be co-supervised by [Hamed Hatami](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~hatami/) and [Robert Robere](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~robere/). I am also fortunate to be part of the Complexity Group at McGill.

Mathematics has always been my first love. I have always enjoyed exploring its many different branches, and over the years I have discovered that I am especially drawn to anything and everything combinatorial.

Before moving to Montreal for my PhD, I completed my undergraduate degree at IIT Delhi. During my time there, I was blessed with wonderful mentors like Professor [Thomas Brüstle](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/nous-joindre/personnel/corps-professoral/professeurs/thomas-brustle) and Professor [Naveen Garg](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~naveen/), who also kindly supervised my undergraduate thesis.

I love thinking deeply, and I enjoy giving myself quiet moments to introspect and philosophize about life. Sometimes those deep thoughts turn into overthinking, and when that happens, I fall back on my family, friends, or a good YouTube rabbit hole. I also find a lot of peace simply wandering through local parks or heading out to catch a beautiful sunrise or sunset.

If you ever want to chat about complexity theory, combinatorics, or exchange notes on the best spots in Montreal to watch the sun go down, please feel free to reach out!
