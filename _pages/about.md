Hello and welcome! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at McGill University, where I am very fortunate to be co-supervised by [Hamed Hatami](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~hatami/) and [Robert Robere](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~robere/) in McGill's Complexity Group.

Mathematics has always been my first love. I enjoy exploring its different branches, but I am especially drawn to problems with a combinatorial flavour. At the moment, I work mostly on questions in computational complexity and learning theory, while still happily exploring where my interests will take me. So far, that curiosity has led me through online algorithms, geometry, representation theory, and persistent homology.

Before moving to Montreal, I completed my undergraduate degree at IIT Delhi. I was fortunate to learn from wonderful mentors such as Professor [Thomas Brüstle](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/nous-joindre/personnel/corps-professoral/professeurs/thomas-brustle) and Professor [Naveen Garg](https://www.cse.iitd.ac.in/~naveen/), who also supervised my undergraduate thesis.

Outside mathematics, I enjoy quiet moments to introspect and philosophize about life. Sometimes that turns into overthinking; when it does, I usually find my way back through family, friends, or a good YouTube rabbit hole. I also love wandering through parks and heading out to catch a beautiful sunrise or sunset.

I am always happy to talk about mathematics, especially when a conversation begins with a genuine question or an interesting idea. Please feel free to reach out—and if the conversation ends with a walk to watch the sunset somewhere in Montreal, even better.
